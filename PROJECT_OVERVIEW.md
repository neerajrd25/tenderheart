# TenderHeart — Business Gist

_by NexusChain Consulting · https://tenderheart.nexuschainconsulting.com_

## In one sentence

A phone-friendly app that lets daycare staff record each child's day and email the parent a
polished daily report with one tap, replacing the paper form.

## The problem it solves

Daycares hand parents a handwritten sheet at pickup: when the child arrived, what they ate, how
long they napped, and so on. Paper is slow to fill in, easy to lose, and looks unprofessional.
TenderHeart digitises that sheet end to end.

## How it works

1. **Staff sign in with Google.** No passwords to manage.
2. **Staff enrol children** with the parent's name and email.
3. **During the day, staff record** for each child:
   - arrival and departure (one-tap Check In / Check Out);
   - breakfast, lunch and evening snacks (how much was eaten, plus a note);
   - nap time (one-tap start and end; the duration is calculated automatically);
   - bowel habits;
   - activities and notes for the parent.
4. **At pickup, staff preview the report and send it.** The parent receives a branded email summary.
5. **At 7 pm, anything complete but unsent goes out automatically.** Incomplete reports are held back for staff to finish.

A dashboard shows the whole day at a glance: how many children are enrolled, in daycare, ready to
send, and sent, plus any reports that need attention.

## Value to the business

- **Saves staff time:** one-tap timestamps and dropdowns instead of handwriting.
- **Looks professional to parents:** a consistent, branded email every day.
- **Nothing gets lost:** every report is stored, not handed over on paper.
- **Low running cost:** hosted on pay-per-use cloud services with no always-on servers.
- **No app store needed:** it runs in the browser and installs to a phone's home screen in one tap.

## Safeguards built in

- A parent is never emailed twice for the same day.
- A report cannot be sent until the essentials (times, meals, bowel habits) are filled in.
- A sent report is locked and cannot be altered.
- A failed email is flagged on the dashboard and can be retried.
- Each staff account sees only its own children.

## What it does not do yet

- Staff cannot share a list of children; each Google login has its own.
- Anyone with a Google account can sign in and get an empty workspace; there is no staff approval step.
- Parents have no login or app; email is the only channel.
- There is no owner/admin view, history browsing or analytics; the dashboard shows today only.
- There is no photo upload, billing, incident log or medication tracking.

## Status

The app is built and runs locally. Going live still needs production sign-in, database and
mailbox credentials, a cloud deployment, and the first real children enrolled.
